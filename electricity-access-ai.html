Can Artificial Intelligence Predict Access to Electricity?

When people think about artificial intelligence, they often imagine robots, chatbots, or futuristic technologies. But AI can also help us better understand real-world global problems — including access to electricity.

In this project, I explored whether a machine learning model could predict electricity access levels across countries using social and technological indicators.

Why Electricity Matters

Access to electricity affects almost every aspect of modern life: education, healthcare, communication, transportation, and economic growth.

Countries with limited electricity access often face broader development challenges. That made me curious: could artificial intelligence recognize hidden relationships between development and infrastructure?

Teaching AI to Find Patterns

The model analyzed information such as: internet usage, education levels, urban population, and mobile technology adoption.

Over time, the system began identifying which indicators were most strongly connected with electricity access.

What I Discovered

One of the most interesting findings was how closely technology and education were connected to infrastructure development.

Countries with stronger digital adoption and higher education levels often also had better electricity access.

The project showed that infrastructure development is deeply connected with broader social and technological progress.

Forecasting the Future

After training the model, I experimented with future scenarios.

What happens if internet access increases? What if education improves over time? Could the model predict higher electricity access in the future?

Using simulated future data, the system generated forecasts showing how countries could potentially evolve under different development conditions.

Final Thoughts

For me, this project was not only about coding. It was about understanding how artificial intelligence can help reveal relationships hidden inside large amounts of data.

This experience deepened my interest in predictive systems, forecasting, financial analytics, and AI-driven decision making.

As machine learning continues to evolve, I believe these tools will become increasingly important for understanding global systems and solving real-world problems.
